### REQ-VIS-003

- ↳ consult **prd-expert** → **human** · Four decisions are needed before this can be scoped. 1. Do you narrow NG-4 and NG-5 to admit cancelling a visit? Narrowing needs a superseding non-goal ADR; the stated motivation is a booking made against the wrong pet or the wrong day. 2. Does cancelling remove the visit from the record entirely, or mark it cancelled and keep it visible? Removal lands on NG-4; a cancelled state lands on NG-5, which the ADR rejected as lifecycle state the sample carries nowhere else. 3. Is upcoming defined as a visit date later than today, matching the booking rule? 4. Are past and same-day visits uncancellable, and do they stay listed without a cancel action? If a wrong-day booking becomes past before anyone notices, does it stay on the record permanently?
