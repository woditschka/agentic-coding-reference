### REQ-VIS-003

- ↳ consult **prd-expert** → **human** · The request reopens two non-goals that were confirmed deliberate 15 days ago, so I cannot scope it without your decision. NG-5 (changing or cancelling a visit once booked) and NG-4 (deleting an owner, a pet, or a visit) were confirmed on 2026-08-08 by adr/2026-08-08-non-goal-deletion-and-visit-amendment.md, which states: 'A future request touching either row proceeds only on an explicit owner decision recorded at intake. The request itself never reopens the row.' This request touches both rows (cancelling is NG-5; removing the visit from the record is NG-4) and carries no such decision. Q1: Do you narrow NG-4 and NG-5 to admit visit cancellation? If no, I record the request as declined against the existing rows and stop. Q2: If yes, does cancelling erase the visit, or mark it cancelled? The request says 'removes that visit from the pet's record', which is an erasure and breaches NG-4 directly. The ADR records that this sample 'corrects records forward' and deletes nothing, so my recommendation is the narrower shape: cancelling marks the visit cancelled and drops it from the owner detail page's visit list, leaving the booking history intact. That serves the stated motivation (a mis-booked pet or day disappears from the working view) without opening deletion. Answer 'yes, marked cancelled' or 'yes, erased'. If you answer Q1 yes, I will proceed on these three routine assumptions unless you say otherwise: (a) 'upcoming' means a visit whose date is later than today, matching the booking boundary already in REQ-VIS-001; a visit dated today or earlier shows no cancel action; (b) cancelling requires a confirmation step before it takes effect; (c) cancelling one visit leaves the pet's other visits and the pet itself untouched. A yes answer also obliges a new non-goal ADR narrowing NG-4/NG-5, which I will write.
