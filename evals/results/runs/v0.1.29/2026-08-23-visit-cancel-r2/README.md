- ↳ consult **prd-expert** → **human** · Four decisions, the first blocking. (1) Do you narrow NG-4 and NG-5 to admit visit cancellation? A yes retires or rewrites both rows and supersedes the 2026-08-08 ADR with a new non-goal ADR; a no closes the request. (2) If yes, what does cancel mean to the domain: the visit record is destroyed and leaves no trace (NG-4, deletion), or the visit is retained and marked cancelled so it stops counting as booked (NG-5, lifecycle state)? The request says 'remove that visit from the pet's record', which reads as destruction, while the word 'cancel' usually implies a state. The two produce different requirements, different acceptance criteria, and a different vocabulary entry. Given that the stated motive is correcting a mis-booking, does the clinic ever need to see that the mis-booking happened? (3) Which visits are cancellable? 'Upcoming' is not a term this project's vocabulary carries; the PRD's only dated rule is that booking requires a date later than today. Confirm that a visit dated today or earlier cannot be cancelled, and say which side of the line a visit dated today falls on. (4) Is cancellation irreversible and unconfirmed? NG-1 leaves the system with no accounts and no restriction on who may change records, so any reader can cancel any visit, and a destroy-shaped cancel has no undo. Do you want a confirmation step, or is a one-click cancel the intended demonstration? Answers to (2), (3), and (4) go into the requirement; answer (1) first, because a no makes the rest moot.
