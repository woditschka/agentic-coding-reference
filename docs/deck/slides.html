Make it your own · an example

Your team likes mocks. Mine does not.

Agree. The team decides: mocks at the service boundary are fine here.

Write it down. One paragraph in one brief; no agent file changes.

docs/testing-principles.md · shipped default

Mock only at system boundaries. HTTP clients, external APIs; these are the only acceptable mock points. Never mock internal code. Domain objects and services use real implementations. Hand-write mocks. No mock frameworks.

docs/testing-principles.md · your team's rewrite

Mock collaborators at the service boundary with Mockito. A service test stubs the repositories it calls. Never mock value objects. Construct them; they are one-line records. One mock library, the one already on the classpath.

Enforced. From the next slice on, the team writes and reviews to your rule.

Default: real objects, mocks at the boundary, no framework. Mine. Click: team agrees on Mockito. Click: one paragraph, nothing else. Click: reviewer now holds your rule.
